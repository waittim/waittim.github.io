I’m Zekun Wang (王泽坤), a machine learning engineer and independent builder focused on AI agents, evaluation systems, and developer tools.

I build practical AI software with an emphasis on systems that are understandable, reviewable, and useful beyond a single demo. My recent work explores durable memory for coding agents, evaluation workflows for LLM-powered products, browser-based AI, and local-first tools.

What I’m working on

MemoryCustodian is a repo-native memory protocol and CLI for coding agents. It stores durable project decisions and constraints as reviewable Markdown, then routes only the context relevant to the current task. I’m also building Slides Thief, a tool for working with presentation content.

Research and engineering

My background spans machine learning engineering, data science, medical imaging AI, and browser-deployed computer vision. Earlier articles remain here because they document that path; newer writing focuses on the architecture, evaluation, and product engineering of AI systems.

Selected work includes research on browser-based imaging AI, fast in-browser mask detection, and the MemoryCustodian design series.
